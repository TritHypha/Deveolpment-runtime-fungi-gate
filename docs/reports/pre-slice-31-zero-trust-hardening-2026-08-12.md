# Pre-Slice-31 Zero-Trust Hardening Report

## Outcome

The reusable safeguards exposed by Slice 30 are implemented before selecting
another TypeScript or MJS source. This is a preparation gate, not a conversion
slice, consumer switch, retirement result, production claim, or permission to
parallelise unknown work.

## Closed safeguards

- Failed package children retain a bounded, digest-bound evidence record with
  exit status, signal, UTF-8 byte length, SHA-256 and capped diagnostics. Raw
  child output remains internal.
- `zt.error-envelope.v1` provides one exact Galerina/SLIDE typed data boundary.
  It contains no message, stack, path, callback, capability, mutable metadata,
  or logger, and `authorityReleased` is always false.
- Every new dated conversion report must carry exactly one skill disposition,
  threadability class, source classification and complete bounded-closure
  statement. Twenty-nine historical reports form a frozen named baseline;
  future reports cannot silently join it.
- Threadability is explicit: pure deterministic leaf compute may be
  `PARALLEL_PURE`; independently scheduled work may be `ASYNC_HAPPY_PATH`;
  a closed service may be `ISOLATED_SERVICE`; shared mutation, active mutable
  compute, admission, signing, synthesis, publication and ordered cleanup stay
  `SERIAL_HARD_PATH`; incomplete evidence is `UNKNOWN` and refuses threading.
- `zt.bounded-closure.v1` validates an exact gate set, evidence digests and
  explicit aggregate exclusions. It launches no command and cannot turn a
  partial bounded check into a repository-wide green claim.
